Sensor Threshold Logic

• Soil Moisture: Low <30% | Moderate 30-60% | High >60%

• Air Humidity: Low <40% | Moderate 40-70% | High >70%

• Temperature: Optimal 18-32°C for most crops

• Rain: Binary (wet/dry) from droplet sensor

• LDR: Shows the raw ESP32 ADC reading (typically 0–4095) and a relative ADC-scale percentage. This is not a calibrated lux measurement; whether the value rises in brighter light depends on your voltage-divider wiring.
